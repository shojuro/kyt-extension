What K.Y.T. does

Cross-platform memory

Conversations from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Claude Code are captured into a single unified memory. Say it once, recall it anywhere.

Automatic context injection

K.Y.T. injects relevant past conversations into your current prompt — automatically. Your AI responds as if it already knows you.

Semantic search

Search across every conversation you've ever had with AI. Vector embeddings + BM25 hybrid search finds meaning, not just keywords.

Three privacy modes

Full memory, Clean Room (search without injection), or Incognito (nothing captured). You decide what gets remembered — and what doesn't.

Data ownership

Export or delete everything. One click. Your data is stored in your own Supabase instance. Nothing leaves your control unless you say so.

Claude Code MCP integration

Query your memory, search entities, and save notes directly from Claude Code. Your terminal remembers everything your browser does.
