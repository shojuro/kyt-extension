Keep Your Thoughts

Your AI conversations, remembered.

K.Y.T. captures your ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini conversations and gives your AI long-term memory across sessions.

  • Ask once, remembered forever — your preferences, facts, and decisions carry across conversations
  • Search your history — find anything you've discussed, instantly
  • You're in control — hide, delete, or export your data anytime

How your data is handled:

  • Never shared with third parties
  • Never used to train AI models
  • Delete everything with one click
